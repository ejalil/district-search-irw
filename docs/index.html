Your Representatives

District boundaries are Maine’s 2021 voting districts, published by Maine GeoLibrary from the redistricting that followed the 2020 Census: U.S. House, State Senate and State House. Addresses are located using OpenStreetMap.

Developed in partnership with Ela Jalil and Aarushi Sahejpal of the Investigative Reporting Workshop, a nonprofit newsroom based at American University.
